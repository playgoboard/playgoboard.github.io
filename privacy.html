Privacy: what we store

GoBoard is a small hobby website. You can play without an account. If you create one, this is what happens with your information.

What we store

  • Your email address and password. Used only to log you in and to send you account emails (confirming your email, or resetting a forgotten password). Your password is stored scrambled (hashed), so nobody can read it, not even the site owner.
  • Your username, rating and number of games, wins and losses. These are public, so other players can see them.
  • Your daily games and finished online games (when you're logged in): opponent, moves and result, so you and others can see your game history and replay games on the Leaderboard page. These are public.
  • Your friends list and friend requests. Only you and the other player can see these.
  • While you search for a Quick match, your game settings and rating (or chosen level) are kept for a few seconds so you can be paired. This is removed when the game starts or you stop searching.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information, and we don't send advertising emails.
  • Chat messages go directly between the two players' browsers and are not stored by us.

Where it is kept

Account information is stored by Supabase, the database service GoBoard uses. Online games are connected with the help of the free PeerJS service. Your browser also remembers some settings (sound on or off, solved puzzles, your Quick match level, language) on your own device, and keeps you logged in. If you uncheck Remember me when logging in, the login is forgotten when the browser closes.

Deleting your account

Log in, open your profile (top right) and choose Delete my account. This removes your account, username, rating, game history and friends list for good.
