How to play

Click a point to place a stone. Surround stones to capture them. Stuck? Hint shows a good move in green. Pass skips your turn, which is normal near the end when nothing useful is left. When both players pass in a row, click any dead groups to remove them, then press Count score. White gets 7.5 bonus points (komi) for moving second.
